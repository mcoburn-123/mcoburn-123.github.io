Why this role

The National MS Society works so that people affected by MS can live their best lives. MS Ambassadors carry that mission in their own voices, from their own lived experience. The director's job is to make sure they're trained, heard in planning and thanked, and that the regional managers who support them have what they need. That's the work I've been doing on a smaller scale, and it's the work I want to do nationally.

Training and tools, in practice: Your First 15 Minutes

A new volunteer usually decides at their first activity whether to come back. The person who controls that moment is the volunteer lead, who's busy. So I built the training for the lead.

What the lead practices, in 3 minutes

  1. Welcome a first-timer with their name, one job and a buddy.
  2. Split a big group into small crews led by returning volunteers.
  3. Give a two-minute safety talk before the work starts.
  4. Close by booking the next date, on the spot.
Play it (3 minutes, works on a phone)
How it would carry over to Ambassadors The scenes here come from a food pantry shift. For Ambassadors, the same format would practice their own moments, written with Ambassadors from their lived experience: a first talk, a first advocacy visit. No login. To measure it: new volunteers active again within 60 days, compared with before. It hasn't been piloted yet; that's the plan.

What I'd do in my first 60 days

A plan for the MS Ambassador Program and the regional volunteer engagement managers, not a promise of results.

Days 1–20 · Listen
  • One-on-ones with each regional manager and the assistant director: what's working, what's stuck, what they want to grow into.
  • Sit in on an Ambassador training and talk with Ambassadors in several regions about what made them stay.
  • Pull a year of data from the volunteer platform: sign-up to first activity, and where people drop off.
Days 21–40 · Map
  • Walk the intake path as a new Ambassador and mark every barrier.
  • Agree with the managers on a short scorecard: time to first activity, 90-day retention, satisfaction.
  • Meet Digital Programs, Marketing, Advocacy and Development to learn what they need from volunteers.
Days 41–60 · Fix one thing
  • Pilot one intake fix in one region and compare it with the others.
  • Start a monthly story pipeline: each region sends one Ambassador story, told the way they want it told.
  • Set a monthly managers' huddle around the scorecard, with an Ambassador voice in the room.
